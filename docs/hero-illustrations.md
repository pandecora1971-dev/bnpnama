# Hero illustrations

Created with the built-in image_gen tool (imagegen skill), not the CLI/API fallback. These are fictional editorial illustrations, not records of real incidents.

## Saved project assets

- public/illustrations/hero-scenes-1.webp — threat, crowd intimidation, exclusion, assault
- public/illustrations/hero-scenes-2.webp — extortion, locked home, bribery, street harassment
- public/illustrations/hero-scenes-3.webp — threatening messages, vandalism, intimidation of a journalist, incident reporting
- public/illustrations/hero-scenes.json — dimensions and individual scene crop bounds

Original PNGs remain in the Codex generated_images directory. WebP assets preserve the generated artwork; the application clips each scene without stretching it. No generated text is used; titles and captions are HTML.

## Shared generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one production sprite sheet for a Bangla civic reporting website's animated hero. Create a precisely aligned 2 by 2 grid of four separate editorial cartoon illustrations. Equal quadrants; each whole illustration contained strictly inside its quadrant with generous 12% empty margins. NO panel borders, NO text, NO captions, NO numbers, NO speech bubbles, NO logos. Uniform flat warm ivory background #f2f0e9 across the entire sheet. Professional hand-drawn editorial illustration, restrained charcoal contour, muted terracotta/coral #FF7873, dusty teal and khaki clothing, subtle natural shading. Adult Bangladeshi people, normal adult proportions (about seven heads tall), three-quarter/side viewpoints, expressive natural faces. Anatomically correct arms: shoulder, elbow and wrist align naturally; palms proportional, natural curled fingers, five fingers when visible; hands must truly grip objects, no mitten hands, no long rubber arms, no detached or reversed wrists. Restrained serious tone, readable real-life scenes, not cute or childish, no floating icons. Each panel has a lightly grounded shadow. Full bodies with feet completely inside quadrant. Consistent linework and characters across all quadrants.
